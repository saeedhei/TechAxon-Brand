# TechAxon Brand Guidelines

## Official Logo
The official TechAxon logo is located in `/logo`.

## Allowed Usage
- Use the logo without modification
- Maintain aspect ratio
- Use transparent background when possible

## Prohibited Usage
- Do not recolor the logo
- Do not stretch or distort the logo
- Do not use the logo for unrelated projects
